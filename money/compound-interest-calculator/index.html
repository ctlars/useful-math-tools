How to use this calculator

Enter the requested values, review any options that affect the calculation, and select Calculate. The main answer appears beside the form, with supporting details below it.

How the calculation works

The balance earns interest at the selected compounding frequency. Each regular contribution is added at the end of its contribution period.

Example

With no contributions, $10,000 at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years grows to about $16,470.09.

Frequently asked questions

When are contributions added?

At the end of each selected contribution period.

Does the rate vary over time?

No. This calculator applies one constant annual rate for the full period.
