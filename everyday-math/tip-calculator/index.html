Tip Calculator

Split a bill and tip across any number of people.

How to use this calculator

Enter the requested values, review any options that affect the calculation, and select Calculate. The main answer appears beside the form, with supporting details below it.

How the calculation works

Tip = bill × tip percentage. The bill and tip are added, then divided evenly by the number of people.

Example

A 20% tip on an $80 bill is $16, making a $96 total or $48 each for two people.
